# Origins

Lilt is an independent adaptation of [px0](https://github.com/px0-ai/px0),
originally created by Arpit Bhayani and distributed under the MIT license.

The initial Lilt codebase was cloned from upstream commit
[`0d2c9d4fc0087a3f045060551e495a2a3192a5d1`](https://github.com/px0-ai/px0/commit/0d2c9d4fc0087a3f045060551e495a2a3192a5d1).
The original copyright and permission notice are retained in [LICENSE](LICENSE),
and the upstream Git history is preserved.

Lilt's name, identity, interface, and landing page are maintained independently
by [Aryan Rathore](https://github.com/aryan-rathore13). The upstream
[`github.com/px0-ai/harness`](https://github.com/px0-ai/harness) Go dependency
remains unchanged.

Historical performance measurements in [BENCHMARKS.md](BENCHMARKS.md) describe
the original upstream project; they are not new Lilt measurements.
